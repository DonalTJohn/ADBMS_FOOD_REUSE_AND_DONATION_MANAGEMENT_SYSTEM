About Food Rescue

Food Rescue & Donation Management System is designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors with organizations that can distribute surplus food to people who need it.

How It Works

01

Donate

Donors register surplus food available for reuse.

02

Request

Organizations submit requests for required food.

03

Match

Available donations are allocated to suitable requests.

04

Deliver

Volunteers pick up and deliver the donated food.
